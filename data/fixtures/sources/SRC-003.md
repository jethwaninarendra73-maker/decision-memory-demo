Deck: Germany surcharge review, steering committee
Date: 2023-06-08

Slide 2: What happened
- The 6% surcharge triggered a competitive response in Germany: a regional competitor held rates and targeted our contract accounts.
- Net quarterly revenue change was -$1.1M versus a +$4.8M plan.

Slide 4: Decision
- Roll the German surcharge back to 2% and exempt high-volume contract customers until their next renewal.
- Expected quarterly uplift after rollback: +$1.2M, with churn loss near 0.5% of baseline.

Slide 5: Lessons
- Do not apply a surcharge to contract customers mid-term.
- Phase by market and review early.
