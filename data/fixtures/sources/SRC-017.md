Deck: North America fuel-linked surcharge review
Date: 2024-07-23

Slide 1: Decision recap
- In April 2024 the US Midwest business introduced an 8% fuel-linked surcharge indexed to a published diesel benchmark.

Slide 2: Result
- US Midwest delivered +$5.4M a quarter against a +$5.0M plan, with churn loss of 0.6% of baseline against a 1.0% plan.

Slide 3: Why it may not transfer
- Index-linked surcharges are standard practice in this market and competitors moved at the same time. European customers have not accepted index-linking.
