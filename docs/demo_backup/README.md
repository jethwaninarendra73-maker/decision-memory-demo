# Backup storyboard

**SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT A PREDICTION OR AUTHORIZED BUSINESS DECISION.**

Still frames of one complete run of the five-minute demo, captured on 2026-11-17 from the running app in its current design (simple view, offline mode, Pricing Director, fresh baseline data). If the laptop or projector fails, open **[slides.html](slides.html)** in any browser (it works offline) and talk over the frames with the **Say** lines from [../DEMO_SCRIPT.md](../DEMO_SCRIPT.md). Arrow keys move between frames; F toggles full screen.

**Show the frames only through `slides.html`.** It keeps the synthetic-data label above every frame. Several frames are scrolled past the app's own label, so opened on their own they would show numbers and advice without it.

They are 640 × 411 screenshots with no sound or motion. They are a fallback, not a recording: a narrated screen recording still has to be made by the presenter ([../DEMO_CHECKLIST.md](../DEMO_CHECKLIST.md)).
