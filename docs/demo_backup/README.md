Still frames of one complete run of the five-minute demo, captured on 2026-11-17 from the running app (offline mode, Pricing Director, fresh baseline data, server under a network guard). If the laptop or projector fails, open **[slides.html](slides.html)** in any browser (it works offline) and talk over the frames with the **Say** lines from [../DEMO_SCRIPT.md](../DEMO_SCRIPT.md). Arrow keys move between frames; F toggles full screen.

**Show the frames only through `slides.html`.** It keeps the synthetic-data label above every frame. Six of the frames were taken scrolled past the app's own label (02, 04, 06, 07, 08 and 10, which include the scenario figures and the recommendation), so opened on their own they would show numbers and advice without it.

They are 800 × 514 screenshots with no sound or motion. They are a fallback, not a recording: a narrated screen recording still has to be made by the presenter ([../DEMO_CHECKLIST.md](../DEMO_CHECKLIST.md)).
